About StoryCast

Stories can change how we understand each other.

StoryCast explores mental health through accessible, carefully framed storytelling. We create space for people to learn at a comfortable pace, question familiar assumptions, and approach complex subjects with greater clarity and care.

The aim is not to simplify people into diagnoses. It is to make trustworthy information easier to understand while keeping dignity, context, and individual difference in view.

01

Our mission

Real voices.
Shared understanding.

Mental health is often discussed through labels, headlines, or assumptions. StoryCast takes a slower approach: combining accessible storytelling with reliable educational context so difficult topics can feel more human and less isolating.

How we approach each story

Careful with the facts.
Careful with people.

Trustworthy mental-health communication depends on both accuracy and respect. These principles guide the educational content published on StoryCast.

01

Check reputable sources

Educational information is checked against established public-health and mental-health sources. Source links are provided so readers can continue learning and review the context directly.

02

Use language that avoids stigma

We avoid sensational framing, harmful shorthand, and language that turns a diagnosis into someone’s whole identity. Experiences are described with care and without assumptions.

03

Be clear about the limits

StoryCast provides general education, not professional diagnosis, treatment, or individual medical advice. Personal health questions belong with a qualified health professional.
